# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Please keep discussion respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent, but be precise about technical concerns.
- Focus feedback on code, design, and observable behavior.
- Respect differing experience levels and communication styles.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or intimidation
- Trolling or sustained hostility
- Publishing private information without permission
- Discriminatory or abusive language

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participation for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately rather than escalating in public issue threads.
